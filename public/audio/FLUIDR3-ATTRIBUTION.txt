FluidR3 General MIDI SoundFont
Original soundfont: Frank Wen, copyright 2000-2002, 2008
MP3 rendering and packaging: Benjamin Gleitzman / MIDI.js Soundfonts

The flute, guitar, and voice MP3 samples were downloaded unchanged from:
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM

Included presets:
- flute-mp3 (public/audio/flute/)
- acoustic_guitar_nylon-mp3 (public/audio/guitar/)
- choir_aahs-mp3 (public/audio/voice/)

Each preset includes C3, Eb3, Gb3, A3, C4, Eb4, Gb4, A4, and C5.
Chordguessr adjusts playback rate for adjacent pitches and applies gain
and duration envelopes at playback time. The MP3 files are unchanged.
